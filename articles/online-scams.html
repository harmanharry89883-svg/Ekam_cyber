How to Stay Safe from Online Scams

The internet makes our lives easier, but it also creates opportunities for scammers. Online scams can target anyone through emails, messages, websites and social media. Understanding common online threats can help you stay safe.

1. Understand Common Online Scams

Online scams are fraudulent activities designed to trick people into sharing personal information, sending money or giving access to their accounts.

Common examples include:

  • Fake shopping websites
  • Phishing emails and messages
  • Fake job and work-from-home offers
  • Lottery and prize scams
  • Impersonation on social media

2. Never Share OTPs or Passwords

Never share your OTP, password, PIN or banking details with anyone. Genuine banks and trusted services will never ask you to reveal your confidential login details.

3. Check Website Links Carefully

Before entering personal information, check the website address carefully. Be careful with links received through unknown emails, SMS messages or social media accounts.

4. Avoid Suspicious Offers

Be careful with offers that promise guaranteed rewards, easy money or unbelievable discounts. Always verify the company and offer through its official website.

5. Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for different accounts. Use a trusted password manager and enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.

6. Keep Your Devices Updated

Install security updates for your phone, computer, browser and applications. Updates help protect devices against known security weaknesses.

7. What to Do If You Encounter a Scam

  • Stop communicating with the suspicious account.
  • Do not send money or share personal information.
  • Change passwords if an account may be compromised.
  • Contact your bank immediately if financial details are affected.
  • Report cyber fraud through official reporting channels.

Conclusion

Staying safe online requires awareness and careful decisions. Always verify suspicious messages, protect your personal information and use secure online practices.
